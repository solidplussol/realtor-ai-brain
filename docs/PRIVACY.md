# PRIVACY

Your Realtor Brain may contain real business information. Treat it carefully.

## Important privacy basics

- your brain may contain business notes, client details, property details, and working knowledge
- for a personal brain, use a **private GitHub repository** — that means a GitHub project folder that is not public
- never put passwords, API keys, Social Security numbers, financial credentials, or other secrets into your brain
- think about your brokerage or company policy before storing client information
- human review still matters for business decisions

## What this means in practice

This starter can help you organize and think, but it is still your responsibility to use judgment about what belongs in the folder and what does not.

If information would be unsafe or inappropriate to store in ordinary notes, do not add it here.
