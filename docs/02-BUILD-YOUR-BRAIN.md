# 02 — BUILD YOUR BRAIN

This is **Part B** of the beginner tutorial.

Time: **about 45–60 minutes**

In this part, Claude first learns how you work, then recommends a brain shape, then waits for your approval before creating your personalized structure.

## 1) Start by typing: `SET ME UP`

Open the Claude **Code** tab with your Realtor Brain folder selected, then type:

`SET ME UP`

That is the cleanest way to start the guided setup.

## 2) What happens next

Claude should walk you through six interview sections in plain English:

1. **Your business, your role, and the kind of work you do most**
2. **The work you want more of and how you decide what is a good fit**
3. **People, partners, and key business contacts**
4. **Your market knowledge, how you describe your value, and how you like to communicate**
5. **How your work gets done day to day**
6. **What you want AI to help with, what it should not do, and what success looks like**

After those core sections, Claude may ask a few extra follow-up questions if it detects that listing work, investment work, or commercial work are a meaningful part of your business.

You do not need perfect answers. Natural answers are fine.

---

## 3) Example questions you may get

Claude should ask conversational questions, not force you into a form. Here are the kinds of questions you should expect.

### Section 1 — Business basics

Examples:

- What does your business look like in one sentence?
- What kind of real estate work do you do most right now?
- Which areas, property types, and client types matter most to you?
- Are you solo, on a team, or leading other people?

### Section 2 — Work focus and fit

Examples:

- What kind of work do you want more of?
- What makes a client, lead, listing, or opportunity a good fit for you?
- What are your red flags or deal-breakers?
- What information do you need before you decide what to do next?

### Section 3 — People and partners

Examples:

- Which people are most important around your business?
- Who do you work with regularly?
- Which vendors, lenders, assistants, or partners matter most?
- Which relationships should your brain help you keep track of?

### Section 4 — Market and communication

Examples:

- Which markets or niches do you know best?
- How do you explain your value to clients?
- What do you want your tone to feel like?
- Are there repeating market ideas or messages you use often?

### Section 5 — Operations

Examples:

- What usually happens from first contact to follow-up?
- Where do things most often get stuck?
- What steps or handoffs happen again and again?
- What do you wish was easier to remember or organize?

### Section 6 — AI goals and boundaries

Examples:

- What would make this brain useful right away?
- Where do you want AI to help first?
- What should AI never do without your confirmation?
- What would success look like a month from now?

---

## 4) How the recap works

After each section, Claude should pause and give you a short recap with three things:

1. **What it heard**
2. **What that means for how it may organize your brain**
3. **What it still needs to know**

That recap is helpful for two reasons:

- you can correct anything Claude misunderstood
- you can see how your answers are shaping the brain before anything is created

If something sounds off, just say so in plain English.

Examples:

- "That part is right, but I do more investor work than buyer work."
- "Change that — I work mostly in Tampa, not St. Pete."
- "Add that I want short answers, not long ones."

---

## 5) How Claude proposes your brain

At the end of the interview, Claude should recommend:

- **one basic starting structure** every realtor gets
- plus **only the extra sections your business actually needs**

This is important:

> Claude should **not** force you into three rigid personas.

Instead, it should say something more like:

- here is the basic structure your brain needs
- here are the extra sections that fit your work

### Basic starting structure

Every brain starts from the same basic foundation:

- People & Relationships
- Properties / Sites / Assets
- Markets & Submarkets
- Partners & Vendors
- Core Playbooks / Rules
- Knowledge / Lessons

### Modules Claude may detect

Depending on your answers, Claude may add one or more modules such as:

- **Listings & Sellers**
- **Investment & Underwriting**
- **Commercial Deal Work**

You might get none, one, two, or all three, depending on your real business.

---

## 6) How to approve or request changes

When Claude shows the recommendation, you do **not** need a special command.

You can approve in normal language, such as:

- "looks good"
- "go ahead"
- "let's do it"
- "yes, build it"

You can also ask for changes first, such as:

- "Make investor work more important."
- "I want less about listings and more about referrals."
- "Change the structure to reflect that I lead a team."

Claude should revise the plan first if you ask for changes.

## 7) What success looks like

After you approve, Claude should create your personalized brain structure in the folder.

You should start seeing new or updated notes and folders appear in the same Realtor Brain folder that is open in Claude, Obsidian, and GitHub Desktop.

Good signs:

- the structure clearly matches how you actually work
- the recommendation makes sense in plain English
- the final shape feels like **your** business, not a generic demo
- you can already imagine adding real clients, properties, notes, or lessons

🎉 **Your Realtor Brain is now created.**
