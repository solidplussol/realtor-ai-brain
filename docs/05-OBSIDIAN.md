# 05 — OBSIDIAN

Obsidian is the reading and browsing view of your brain.

## What a vault means

In Obsidian, a **vault** is simply a folder of notes opened inside Obsidian.

For this project, your vault is your Realtor Brain folder.

## How to browse your brain

Start with the file list on the left side of the Obsidian window. That is where you can see your folders and notes.

Click through them the same way you would browse files in a normal folder.

## What links do

Some notes may link to other notes.

That means you can move from one idea to another by clicking those links, for example:

- from a person to a property
- from a property to a market note
- from a market note to a lesson or playbook

## How to search

If you want to find something quickly, use Obsidian's built-in search feature (usually a magnifying glass icon) and type a word, name, address, or phrase.

That is often the fastest way to confirm whether a note already exists.

## How to see new information after Claude updates files

After Claude creates or updates notes:

- return to Obsidian
- look in the file list on the left side of the Obsidian window for new or changed note names
- click into the notes to read what changed

If you are not sure whether anything changed, also check GitHub Desktop's **Changes** tab (the list of files that changed). That will show whether files were updated.

You do **not** need plugins to get started.
