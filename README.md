# Realtor AI Brain

This starter helps a realtor build a personal AI-supported brain in a folder on their computer. It is for keeping track of how you work, what you know, and the people, properties, markets, and notes that matter to your business.

## What problem does it solve?

A lot of real estate knowledge gets scattered across texts, notes, files, and memory. The Realtor AI Brain brings that into one place so Claude can help you organize it, update it, and use it later.

## Two core ideas

- **KNOW ME** — Claude learns who you are, what kind of real estate work you do, how you make decisions, how you like to communicate, and what matters most in your business.
- **KNOW WHAT I KNOW** — your brain stores your knowledge in organized notes so Claude can connect it, reuse it, and help you ask better questions later.

## Public template vs. your private brain

This GitHub repository is the **PUBLIC template** — an empty starting point that does **not** contain anyone's real clients, deals, contacts, or business knowledge.

Your actual **PRIVATE brain** is the personal copy you make from this template and fill in with your own notes, relationships, files, and real business information.

## The tools

- **Claude** — helps build and use your brain
- **Obsidian** — lets you browse and read your brain
- **GitHub Desktop** — helps you save versioned changes on your computer
- **GitHub** — keeps the online copy after you push

> ⚠️ This will hold real business/client information — use a **PRIVATE repository** (a GitHub project folder that is not public). See **[Privacy](docs/PRIVACY.md)**.
